About the Butters Family

The Butters family has farmed the land outside Sandwich, Illinois for generations. In late 2004, the family decided to open up part of the farm to visitors, adding a few rides and a picnic area for the neighborhood. What started as a small weekend attraction grew into Butter Family Farms Amusement Park, now a local favorite for family outings.

The park is run day-to-day by the Butters family under their company, Butter Family Office. Every ride, snack stand, and gift shop shelf is looked after personally by a member of the family or someone who feels like one.

After years of steady growth, the family is now working with investors on the Bigger & Butter Expansion Project — a plan to bring the Butter Family Farms experience to new locations, with Orlando and Zubda under consideration for the next park.

Whatever comes next, the mission stays the same: simple, affordable, family-run fun. Butter Times with Butter People.
