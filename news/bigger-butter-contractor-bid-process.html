BFF Opens Competitive Bid Process for Bigger & Butter Construction Partner

April 17, 2025 · Company News | Bigger & Butter Project

Sandwich, Illinois — April 17, 2025

Butter Family Farms has formally opened a competitive bidding process to select a construction and theming contractor for the first phase of the Bigger & Butter Project, the company announced Thursday.

The park issued a Request for Proposal (RFP) to a shortlist of qualified firms with experience in theme park construction, ride infrastructure, and large-scale entertainment venue development. Interested contractors were invited to submit proposals through May 9, 2025.

COO Buttercup Butters, who is leading the construction partner selection process, said the search prioritized firms with a demonstrated track record in amusement and entertainment construction, strong safety compliance records, and the operational capacity to deliver against an aggressive build timeline.

"We are not looking for the biggest firm," Butters said. "We are looking for the right partner. Someone who understands what BFF is, what our guests expect, and what it means to build something people will bring their families to for the next twenty years."

The RFP outlined a phased construction scope including site preparation, ride foundation and mechanical installation, back-of-house infrastructure, network and security systems integration, and theming. Firms were required to provide relevant project case studies, safety records, bonding and insurance documentation, and a preliminary project timeline.

Update: June 2, 2025

Three firms are understood to have submitted proposals by the May 9 deadline. Following an internal review process led by Buttercup Butters and Lucas Bigheart, Butter Family Farms announced today that Prairie Build Group of Naperville, Illinois had been selected as the Bigger & Butter Project's primary construction and theming contractor.

"Prairie Build Group stood out immediately," Butters said. "Their portfolio speaks for itself and their team asked exactly the right questions during the process. We are confident we have found our partner."

Prairie Build Group, a construction and theming firm with offices in Naperville, has provided no public statement on the engagement. The firm's project portfolio includes entertainment venue construction, themed environment buildout, and infrastructure work for regional attraction operators across the Midwest.

Construction planning and site assessment work is expected to begin in Q3 2025, with a formal groundbreaking anticipated before the end of the calendar year pending final investment close.

Full details on the Bigger & Butter Project timeline will be shared with guests and stakeholders as milestones are reached.
