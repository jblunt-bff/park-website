Butter Family Farms Strengthens Security Posture Ahead of Expansion Milestone

May 22, 2025 · Company News

Sandwich, Illinois — May 22, 2025

As Butter Family Farms prepares for the most significant period of growth in its two-decade history, the park announced this week that it has completed a comprehensive external security assessment of its digital and physical infrastructure — a move its Head of Security called "overdue and necessary."

The assessment, conducted by an external penetration testing firm retained earlier this year, covered BFF's production network environment, physical access controls, ride operational technology systems, and guest-facing digital platforms including the park's mobile app, ticketing infrastructure, and point-of-sale systems.

"We have a twenty year old park with twenty year old habits in some areas," said Robin McGrass, BFF's Head of Security. "When you are preparing to bring in outside investors and expand to new markets, you cannot afford gaps. We wanted to know exactly where we stood before anyone else did."

McGrass said the assessment identified a number of findings across priority levels, all of which are currently in active remediation tracked through the park's internal security ticketing process. He declined to characterize specific findings but confirmed that no guest data or financial systems were found to have been compromised.

The engagement is part of a broader security investment BFF has made in the past eighteen months, which has included the hiring of IT Security Analyst Karen Ellis, network hardening work led by Network Administrator Jasmine Blunt, the expansion of the park's vulnerability management program, and upgrades to physical access control systems across the campus.

"Karen and Jasmine have done serious work getting our environment to where it needs to be," McGrass said. "This assessment validated a lot of that progress and gave us a clear picture of what still needs attention."

The security push is timed to coincide with the investor audit phase of the Bigger & Butter Project, during which outside firms conducting due diligence will review BFF's operations, financials, and systems in detail.

"Investors are going to look at everything," said Lucas Bigheart, Director of Finance and Investor Relations. "Having a clean, documented security posture is part of showing them this is a serious, well-run organization."

BFF expects to complete primary remediation efforts by late summer 2025, ahead of a planned investor review in the fall.
