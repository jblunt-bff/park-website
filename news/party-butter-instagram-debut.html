Say Hello to Party Butter: BFF's Official Mascot Makes His Instagram Debut

June 24, 2025 · Park News | Community

Sandwich, Illinois — June 24, 2025

Butter Family Farms has a new face, and he is smooth, golden, and extremely online.

The park officially introduced Party Butter — BFF's long-awaited official mascot — to the world this week, alongside the launch of his Instagram account at @party.butter. The account went live Monday morning and gained over 400 followers in its first 24 hours, which park staff described as "honestly more than we expected" and Party Butter himself described as "not enough."

Party Butter is a six-foot anthropomorphic stick of butter with a party hat, a permanent grin, and what the park's communications team calls "a lot of opinions about roller coasters." He has been a fixture at BFF events and private appearances for the past year, but Monday marked his first official public presence online and the formal launch of his role as the park's brand ambassador.

"Party Butter has been wandering around this park making guests smile for a while now," said COO Buttercup Butters. "It felt like time to let the rest of the world meet him. He has been very patient."

Party Butter was not available for direct comment at press time, as he was reportedly in the BBC queue. A representative speaking on his behalf said he was "excited, emotional, and a little nervous about the drop."

The Instagram

The @party.butter account launched with a grid of images from Party Butter's first official day on the job, including photos from the BBC load platform, a cameo at Sweet Churns where he was photographed eating what appeared to be an entire caramel apple in one go, and a video of him attempting the Mine Train Coaster with mixed results.

The account will feature regular park updates, behind the scenes content, seasonal promotions, and what the park promises will be Party Butter's completely unfiltered takes on things happening at BFF.

"He has a voice," said Kathleen Fairweather, BFF's Social Media Specialist, who manages the account. "It took us a little while to find it. But once we did, it was very obvious. Very him."

Fairweather added that the account would also be the first place guests hear about promotions, discount codes, and seasonal events before they are announced anywhere else.

"If you are not following Party Butter, you are going to miss things," she said. "That is just the reality."

Who is Party Butter?

According to official BFF lore, Party Butter was born in the Sandwich, Illinois cold storage facility of an unnamed dairy cooperative on a Tuesday, which he describes as "honestly fine." He discovered his love of theme parks at a young age, applied to work at BFF in 2023, and was turned down twice before Darius Butters personally intervened.

"I saw something in him," Darius Butters said. "Also he would not stop showing up."

Party Butter's hobbies include riding the BBC (front row only), sampling every item at Sweet Churns (professionally, he insists), and standing near the exit of the Coffee Cups to observe guests' reactions. His dislikes are listed on his Instagram bio as "margarine, lukewarm funnel cake, and people who do not put their trays away."

He holds no formal qualifications but considers himself a subject matter expert in having a good time.

Following Party Butter

Guests are encouraged to follow @party.butter on Instagram for park updates, promotions, and content that Kathleen Fairweather describes as "brand approved but spiritually free range."

Party Butter will also be available for meet and greets at BFF on weekends throughout the season. Scheduling and location updates will be posted to the Instagram account in real time.

"Come find me," Party Butter said, via representative. "I will be near the churros."
