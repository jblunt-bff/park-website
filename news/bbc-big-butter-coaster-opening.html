The BBC is Open: Butter Family Farms Debuts Its Most Ambitious Ride Yet

September 15, 2024 · Park News | Rides

Sandwich, Illinois — September 15, 2024

After two years of construction, planning, and more than a few sleepless nights, Butter Family Farms officially opened the BBC — the Big Butter Coaster — on Saturday morning to a crowd of guests who had been waiting at the gate since before sunrise.

The BBC is a LIM launched roller coaster manufactured by DropForge Attractions. Its 120-foot top hat is the tallest ride structure in the park's twenty-year history, and the coaster is one of the most significant capital investments BFF has ever made. Linear induction motors launch sixteen riders per train from zero to 60 miles per hour in under three seconds and send them straight up the top hat, where they hang at the crest for a moment — long enough, several guests noted, to sincerely reconsider their choices — before plunging back down toward magnetic brakes at the station.

Total ride time: approximately ninety seconds. Recovery time: variable.

"I cried a little bit," said one guest shortly after her first ride. "I am going again."

Darius Butters cut the ribbon at 9:00 AM alongside Ride Operator Anthony Zamora, who will serve as the BBC's primary operator, and Head of Security Robin McGrass, who confirmed that the ride had passed all required pre-opening safety inspections and third-party certifications.

"DropForge builds an exceptional product," Butters said. "We are proud to have this in Sandwich."

The BBC joins the Corkscrew Roller Coaster, Wooden Roller Coaster, Mine Train Coaster, River Rapids, Haunted House, and Log Flume as headline attractions at BFF. The ride is included with general admission.

Height requirement: 48 inches. Courage requirement: entirely personal.

Tickets are available at butterfamilyfarms.com.
