Butter Family Farms Announces the Bigger & Butter Project: A Bold Expansion Into New Markets

March 4, 2025 · Company News

Sandwich, Illinois — March 4, 2025

Butter Family Farms, the beloved Midwest theme park that has called Sandwich, Illinois home for over twenty years, today announced the Bigger & Butter Project — an ambitious initiative to expand the BFF brand from one park to potentially three locations across the United States and beyond.

Founders Darius and Buttercup Butters made the announcement in a letter to guests published on the park's website and social channels Tuesday morning, calling it "the most exciting chapter in BFF's history."

"We opened these gates in October 2004 with one ride, twelve employees, and a popcorn machine that broke on day two," Darius Butters wrote. "What we also had was a belief that families deserve something extraordinary right in their own backyard. Twenty years later, we know we were right. Now we want to share that with families who do not live in Sandwich."

The Bigger & Butter Project targets expansion into two new markets, with Orlando, Florida and an international location under active consideration. The Butters family is currently in conversations with outside investment firms and expects to announce a lead investor partner before the end of the year.

Lucas Bigheart, the park's Director of Finance and Investor Relations, confirmed that financial due diligence packages have been prepared and that investor conversations are "at an advanced stage."

"BFF has a twenty year track record of consistent guest growth, a loyal community, and an operational model that travels," Bigheart said. "The fundamentals are strong. We are excited about what the next chapter looks like."

The expansion project will include new ride development, with flagship experiences planned for each new location. The park has not announced whether new locations will mirror BFF's existing lineup or introduce original concepts exclusive to each market.

Existing BFF guests in Sandwich were quick to respond, with the announcement drawing hundreds of comments on social media within hours.

"BFF was my first job. Now I bring my own kids. Whatever you build next, we will show up," wrote one commenter.

The Sandwich location will remain the flagship park and will not reduce operations during the expansion period, the company confirmed.

More information on the Bigger & Butter Project, including investor relations contacts, is available at butterfamilyfarms.com.
