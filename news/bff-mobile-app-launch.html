Butter Family Farms Launches Official Mobile App for the 2025 Season

June 10, 2025 · Park News | Technology

Sandwich, Illinois — June 10, 2025

Butter Family Farms officially launched its guest-facing mobile application this week, bringing together park maps, ride reservations, food ordering, loyalty rewards, and ticket management in a single platform designed to make the BFF experience smoother from the parking lot to the exit gate.

The app, available for iOS and Android, was built to support the park's growing guest base ahead of the Bigger & Butter Project expansion and introduces several features that BFF guests have long requested.

Key features of the BFF app include:

  • Ride Photo Purchase: Guests can view and purchase photos taken on the BBC and other equipped rides directly through the app, eliminating physical kiosk queues.
  • Mobile Tickets & Pass Management: Season passes and single-day tickets are stored digitally and scannable at all entry points.
  • Real-Time Park Map: A live map shows current wait times, food stand locations, event schedules, and accessible route options.
  • Food Pre-Ordering: Guests can order from participating food locations inside the park and pick up without waiting in line.
  • BFF Loyalty Program: Every purchase earns Butter Points redeemable for discounts, merchandise, and priority access perks.
  • Emergency Notifications: App users can receive real-time alerts for weather, park closures, and emergency information.

COO Buttercup Butters, who led the app development initiative, said the goal was to reduce friction for guests without losing the warmth that defines BFF's reputation.

"We are a family park, not a tech company," Butters said. "The app should feel like it is helping, not in the way. Every feature we built had to answer one question: does this make the guest's day better?"

The app is available for free download. Existing season pass holders can link their passes by logging in with their BFF account credentials. New users can create an account during onboarding.

Download links are available at butterfamilyfarms.com/app.
